Privacy Policy — Arrow Shapeshifter

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Arrow Shapeshifter ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Arrow Shapeshifter is a puzzle game published by NN Studios.

In short: the App does not ask for your name, email, or any personal account details. Your game progress is stored on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your progress can also be backed up to your Google account. The App shows ads and offers in-app purchases, both handled by Google — see below for what that means for your data.

1. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device:

  • Your level progress, stars earned, unlocked themes, token balance, and collection progress
  • Your app settings (sound, vibration, language)

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted if you uninstall the App.

Information handled by Google Play Games Services (only if you choose to sign in):

  • Your Google account ID, used to save and restore your progress across devices
  • Achievements and leaderboard data, if applicable

Signing in is optional. If you don't sign in, your progress stays local to your device only.

Information handled by Google AdMob (advertising):

  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data, used to show and measure ads
  • Depending on your device and region, ads may be personalized based on your interests, or non-personalized

Information handled by Google Play Billing (in-app purchases):

  • Purchase transactions are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but we never see or store your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.)

3. Children's Privacy

Arrow Shapeshifter is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can remove it.

4. Data Retention

Local progress data is retained on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Cloud save data (if you sign in with Google Play Games) is retained by Google according to your Google account settings, and can be deleted by unlinking the App from your Google account or contacting Google.

5. Your Choices

  • You can play without signing in to Google Play Games — your progress simply won't sync across devices.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization").
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to remove all locally stored data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
